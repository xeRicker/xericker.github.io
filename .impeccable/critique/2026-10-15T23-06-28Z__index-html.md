## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment (unanchored, judged before detector output):** genuinely authored, not category-interchangeable. The revenue model is the business - Utarg / Karty / Glovo with a gold Glovo variant and a guard that refuses card + glovo > utarg before writing. The product grid uses insider grammar (Mieso: Male, Sos: Carolina, Zboj: Czosnek) and carries a rule no generic inventory app would ship: Bulki at 0 prints as an X. The Burgery tab is an instrument (fat retention by doneness, oil absorption by draining method, fryable fried/airfryer toggle, presets up to Bydlak), and the terminal artefact is a Polish Messenger list, not a dashboard. The one weakly-authored element is the icon-only category header - a generic icon grid grafted onto a very specific inventory.

**Deterministic scan:** 2 findings, exit 2, both false positives proven by controlled experiment. The detector claims h1 renders rgb(0,0,0) and that all roles are 16px (step 1.00:1). The live page computes h1 = rgb(212,82,26) / 24px, h2 18px, body 14px - real steps of 1.29x and 1.33x. Cause: the detector does not follow @import, and index.html links only style.css whose 15 local sheets are @imports. document.styleSheets in the live page returns 2 entries while all 16 sheets are applied. Minimal reproductions confirmed it: the same declarations via <style> or <link> scan clean (exit 0); wrapped in one @import they reproduce the finding byte-for-byte. These two rules are permanent noise on this repo until suppressed.

**Visual overlays:** attempted and failed. The browser channel was unavailable to both assessments, so no overlay was injected and none exists. Fallback signal: measured computed styles, AX tree, hit-box geometry and CDP screenshots.

## Overall Impression

A genuinely well-built craft surface with a real accessibility hole and a correctness hole underneath it. The visual system holds up under measurement - every contrast pair passes except the ember-fill ones, focus rings are present on every keyboard-focused control, no horizontal overflow at 390/1264/1400px, and prefers-reduced-motion genuinely switches the entrance animations off. But two things break the product's own promise: a keyboard or screen-reader user cannot record any of the 40 toggle products at all, and a failed catalog read is silently indistinguishable from a good one. The biggest opportunity is the closing flow itself - the most consequential action in the app is labelled "copy", it writes a real file, and the user never sees what was recorded or which day it lands on.

## What's Working

1. The accessibility plumbing that is applied is careful and verified live: all 44 buttons and 18 textboxes have accessible names; icon-only controls carry explicit labels; every decorative glyph is aria-hidden; danger dialogs focus cancel, not confirm; focus is trapped in sheets and restored to the opener; the tablist exposes aria-selected/aria-controls/role="tabpanel".
2. Focus and motion are real, not nominally declared: a measured 2px #E76A32 ring on every keyboard-focused control (settled, after the 0.2s transition), and under emulated prefers-reduced-motion: reduce getAnimations() returns 0 with opacity 1 / transform none - the 78 load animations are genuinely disabled.
3. The revenue card and burger configurator are product-specific instruments. Money fields are 24px right-aligned tabular with an in-field PLN prefix; the Glovo variant focuses in gold. Macros reuse the palette semantically (kcal ember, protein blue, fat gold, carbs green).

## Priority Issues

**[P0] A keyboard or screen-reader user cannot record toggle-type products.**
- What: 40 products render as div.card.product-card.type-toggle with no role, no aria-checked/aria-pressed, tabIndex -1, and their checkbox set to display:none. Measured: el.focus() does not move focus off body; the AX tree exposes 0 checkbox nodes. Corroborated independently from mainRender.js:120-130 and the click-only handler at main.js:162-171.
- Why it matters: Chorizo, Pomidory, Sos: Carolina, Torby, Drobne are unrecordable without a mouse, and nothing warns the user - the report comes out incomplete. Silent data loss on the daily path.
- Fix: make the card a real button role="switch" aria-checked, or keep the checkbox visually-hidden-but-focusable with a :focus-visible ring on the card and a label from the product name.
- Command: harden

**[P0] A failed catalog read is silently indistinguishable from a real one.** (carried from the previous polish pass; not re-tested here.)
- What: api.js never throws on reads - it console.warns and returns null (lines 88, 301) - and the loaders substitute hardcoded defaults: seven real staff names in DEFAULT_EMPLOYEES (employees.js:3), a legacy product set, and Osweicim/Osiek. This run's evidence is consistent: zero console output, zero failed requests, zero api.github.com calls, and no #globalLoader element, so there is no state in which the user is told a read failed.
- Why it matters: a shift can be closed against a stale catalog where a newly added employee or product is not there.
- Fix: keep the resilience, make the fallback visible - the three loaders report that they fell back, and a noticeService warning with a retry on both surfaces.
- Command: harden

**[P1] The report date is invisible and unchangeable, but the file is written to it.**
- What: getFormattedDate() is used silently; no date appears on the Lista tab or in Wybierz punkt (the sheet holds exactly two location buttons). A shift closing after midnight files under the next day, and burbone_state expires at local end-of-day.
- Why it matters: wrong-day files corrupt the owner's per-day totals, with no recovery.
- Fix: show "Raport: Osweicim, 16.10.2026" in the Lista header and location sheet, and make it editable with the custom calendar the admin already uses.
- Command: harden

**[P1] The write is hidden behind a "copy" label, and the record is never previewed.**
- What: the FAB Skopiuj liste opens the sheet; a second Skopiuj copies; the GitHub write happens silently in between. The generated text is never displayed. The overwrite confirm names neither the day, the location, nor what is lost.
- Why it matters: staff cannot verify what they hand to the team, cannot tell that a business file was written, and may close the tab believing nothing was stored.
- Fix: rename the flow to its consequence (Zakoncz zmiane -> summary sheet with location, date, totals and the full list text -> Zapisz i skopiuj), reusing the admin's preview panel; name the day and location in the overwrite confirm.
- Command: clarify

**[P2] Nothing on this surface helps you recognise or find things.**
- What: icon-only category headers (mainRender.js:112-115 renders the icon and no name, so meat's cue is egg_alt); the macro table's four value th elements contain only aria-hidden icons so the columns announce blank, and macro chips carry only a container aria-label; a 55-card single scroll across 11 sections with no search, filter, jump or sticky nav; a 96-option time grid (every 15 minutes, 3 columns, 320px popover) competing with 17 quick presets - more than 4 options at both decision points.
- Why it matters: unlearnable labels and scroll-hunting on a phone at the truck, in the dark, against a two-minute promise.
- Fix: print the section name beside the icon; add visually-hidden text to each macro chip and th; add sticky category jump chips or a filter above the product grid; make the time picker hour-then-minute with a "teraz" shortcut.
- Command: distill

## Persona Red Flags

**Sam (screen reader + keyboard).** Cannot toggle any of the 40 toggle products - no focus, no role, no state, no checkbox in the AX tree, so the report is silently incomplete. The macro table's four value columns announce as blank and macro chips read as one undifferentiated string. The tablist announces itself as a tablist but implements none of the tabs keyboard pattern (no Arrow keys, no roving tabindex - setupTabs binds click only), and the save-in-progress text swap is not a live region, so "Zapisuje..." is never announced. Reaching the FAB costs ~90 Tab presses.

**Jordan (confused first-timer).** The category headers are unexplained icons; Bulki = 0 -> X is documented nowhere; nothing says the form is only local until the FAB is pressed. Two stacked "copy" steps with a hidden write between them gives no model of what happened, and the success sheet offers no way out except copying.

**Marta, the closing shift lead** (project-specific, from PRODUCT.md's primary audience: phone at the truck, low light, two minutes). Fails at three points: she cannot verify the day the report will be filed under, she cannot see the list before handing it off, and she searches a 55-card column on her feet to find Frytki.

- ::placeholder is unthemed - the revenue fields use Chrome's UA gray rgb(117,117,117) (3.40:1 on the raised input; passes at 24px but is off-palette). One line fixes it and matches the "theme the browser's surfaces" rule.

- Unchecked toggle fill measures 1.09:1 against the card, but its 1px border is 3.45:1 - the boundary is perceivable, so this is not a defect; flagged only so it is not "fixed" later.

## Questions to Consider

1. reportFormatter.js emits location, date, staff and products - never Utarg, Karty or Glovo. If the takings never reach Messenger, why does the closing flow make a worker type three amounts under time pressure with no preview and no on-screen confirmation they were recorded?
2. When a report for the day already exists, is yes/no overwrite the right question - or should the second person see the first list rather than trust a dialog?
3. Would "copy yesterday's list" be worth more to a closing shift than the entire Burgery tab? If so, which one belongs on the phone at the truck?
4. The same field offers a 17-option preset and a 96-option grid. Which is the intended path - and does a 96-row popover earn its keep on a phone?
