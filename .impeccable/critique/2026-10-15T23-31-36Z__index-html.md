## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment (unanchored):** the mechanics are unmistakably this business - no other product ships Utarg/Karty/Glovo with derived cash, a gold Glovo field, presets of exactly 12:00/14:00/16:00 against 12:00-21:00 and 14:00-20:00, one-shift workers that are never persisted, and a rule that Bulki = 0 prints as X. What is generic is the shell (header card, pill tabs, stacked cards, one ember FAB) - and that shell is what the page ends on: buildReportText's output is never rendered anywhere on this surface, while the admin renders exactly that text in a preview. A screen that writes a business record without displaying it reads as data entry, not reporting.

1. The custom-control layer is careful and measured: native select/time/date stay focusable-but-invisible with their aria-label preserved, the visible proxy carries that name, popovers are width-clamped and flipped when there is no room below, Escape closes them.

3. The write path is verified rather than trusted: a failed save still hands over the list with a specific Polish message, a clipboard failure raises a danger toast with a manual fallback, and production saves are re-read and byte-compared instead of trusting a 200.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] Keyboard activation of a product toggle is a no-op - the previous fix is incomplete.**
- What: B's trusted key trace: Space x2 and Enter on #checkbox-Chorizo left checked: false. The capture listener sees true; the app's handleProductClick (main.js:180-188) then sets checked = !checked and dispatches a change, undoing the native toggle. A mouse click on the card still works.
- Why it matters: the 40 toggle products remain unoperable by keyboard, so the earlier P0 is only cosmetically closed - a screen-reader user can find Chorizo and hear its state but cannot change it.
- Fix: in handleProductClick, return early when e.target.type === 'checkbox'; the native toggle already happened and handleProductChange updates the card. One line, no behaviour change for mouse users.
- Command: harden

**[P1] The record is never shown, and the write is never receipted.**
- What: buildReportText's output goes straight to the clipboard inside the success sheet; index.html has no preview element. Nothing after a save states the truck, the date, or that a file was written.
- Why it matters: the staff member authors a record the owner trusts without re-adding, and it silently replaces an existing file, yet they cannot read one character of it before or after.
- Fix: make the success sheet a receipt - an identity line (Zapisano: Oswiecim - 16.10.2026) above a read-only scrollable block, reusing the admin's admin-list-copy-preview pattern, with Skopiuj liste as the single action.
- Command: clarify

**[P1] The day and the truck are invisible, and the destructive confirm names neither.**
- What: date is computed and never rendered; selectedLocation is module state. The confirm reads "Lista z tego dnia juz istnieje. Nadpisac?" with no date and no truck - and the truck is chosen after the save tap.
- Why it matters: two trucks share one phone; the two facts that determine which file is overwritten are the two the screen never states. After midnight the same code writes the next day's file.
- Fix: a persistent identity row under the header (two pills plus the editable date via the existing custom calendar), and pass both into the overwrite dialog.
- Command: harden

**[P1] Quantity cards are false affordances.**
- What: .product-card:active (scale + background) applies to every card, and cursor:pointer/user-select:none are set on all of them, but handleProductClick only acts on .btn-qty and toggle cards. For about 22 quantity rows the whole card presses and nothing happens; the real targets are the 40x40 circles (measured 40x40 at all three widths).
- Why it matters: the largest, most inviting target on the row is inert, while the working targets are the smallest in the app at the moment of highest tap frequency.
- Fix: make the card body increment for quantity rows, or drop the card-level :active and bring the steppers to the 44px standard.
- Command: layout

**[P2] The markup does not carry the meaning the design claims.**
- Nested cards: 4 x class="card employee-row" inside card card--section - banned by both AGENTS.md and DESIGN.md.
- Unlabelled groups: the 12 category headers render only an aria-hidden icon, so the meat group's cue is egg_alt and assistive tech receives no grouping.
- Blank column headers: the Makro table's four value th elements are icon-only and aria-hidden, so kcal/protein/fat/carbs announce as empty.
- Missing tabular figures: font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums is absent from burgers.css (0 of 16 occurrences), so the Makro tiles and their columns are the only comparable numbers in proportional digits.
- Command: distill
